Dodo Payments is emailing your licence key to the address you used at checkout. It usually arrives within a minute; check your spam folder if it doesn't.

Activate in three steps

  1. Open Dispatch Desk

    Click its icon in Chrome's toolbar, or press Alt+Shift+D.

  2. Open Settings

    Click the settings button at the top right of the panel and scroll to Licence.

  3. Paste the key and press Activate

    The tag at the top changes to PRO and the limits are lifted straight away.

Installed

Dispatch Desk is ready. Your first packet takes about five minutes.

Save a carrier once, then fill broker setup forms in one click and check a broker's FMCSA record before you book.

Get going in three steps

Pin it first, so it's one click away on every load.

  1. Pin and open it

    Click the puzzle-piece icon in your browser's toolbar and pin Dispatch Desk. Open it with its icon or Alt+Shift+D: it opens beside the page you're on.

  2. Add your carrier

    Company, MC and DOT, contacts, insurance and equipment. Tax ID and bank details are encrypted with a passphrase only you know.

  3. Fill a broker packet

    On RMIS, MyCarrierPackets and Highway press the green Fill form button. On any other setup form, right-click it and choose Fill form with carrier, or press Alt+Shift+F. Check, then submit yourself.

Checking a broker: highlight an MC or DOT number on any page, right-click and choose Vet with Dispatch Desk. You get authority status, bond and insurance on file, out-of-service orders and safety rating from FMCSA.

A form didn't fill?

Tell us which broker's setup page it was and we'll add it. Most fixes ship within a week, and we'll email you when yours is done.
